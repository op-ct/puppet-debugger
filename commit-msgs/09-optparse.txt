Replace the vendored Trollop with optparse

lib/puppet-debugger/trollop.rb was an 869-line copy of Trollop 2.x used
by Cli.start for six options. Cli.parse_options now builds the same
options with OptionParser from the standard library: --play, --facts,
--run-once, --node-name, --catalog and --quiet, each with the one-letter
short form Trollop derived, plus --help and a new --version. Remaining
arguments are left in ARGV for the FILE handling as before. A bad option
prints the error and exits 255 as Trollop did.

Visible change: the layout of `puppet-debugger --help`.
